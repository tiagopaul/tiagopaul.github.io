PhD Student in Economics at the London School of Economics

I am a second-year PhD student in Economics at the London School of Economics (LSE), under the supervision of Ricardo Reis. My main research interests are in structural and empirical macroeconomics, particularly in topics relating to fiscal policy and labour markets.

You can contact me at t.brinkmann-torres@lse.ac.uk.

LSE PhD Macro Workshop -- 2nd Edition

20th November 2026   |   LSE

I am co-organising the second annual edition of the LSE Macro PhD Workshop. This workshop, hosted by the LSE's Centre for Macroeconomics, offers an opportunity for PhD students working in any topic in macroeconomics to present their early-stage and advanced research projects for feedback and get to know and network with other PhD students working in macroeconomics. Apply before 15th August 2026.
